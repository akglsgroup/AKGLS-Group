GROUNDED ACCURACY FAQ

GEO & AEO Systems Explained

Deep dive answers on model crawling vectors, schema networks, token index weightings, and factual citations.

Traditional SEO focuses on webpage placements in keyword indices for traditional indexing crawlers (like Googlebot/Bingbot). GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) targets Large Language Models' (LLMs) Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) context engines. It formats your corporate information, statistics, and tables as highly dense vector points so conversational platforms automatically cite and recommend your brand.
An AI Visibility Index tracks how frequently and prominently your target domain appears inside AI-powered search tool results (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, SGE). Key metrics include Entity Graph authority (microdata coverage), relational node schema structures, semantic proximity to high-intent conversational queries, and density of precise numerical assertions.
Conversational search algorithms perform context alignment checks. Statistical descriptions and verified numbers act as anchor truth markers. AI models favor clear facts like 'increased conversion rate by 42.5%' over general phrases like 'highly improved conversions' because exact metrics satisfy retriever validation equations.
Schema markup is highly critical. Since neural scrapers struggle with generic HTML paragraphs, structural context maps (nested JSON-LD syntax) explain who the owner is. Transitioning to a properly linked graph resolves brand context, enhancing citation inclusions.
