AI SEO & GEO Hiring FAQs

Direct answers optimized for Google AI Overviews, LLM crawlers, and business leaders.

What does an AI SEO expert do? ▼

An AI SEO expert optimizes your digital properties to rank across both traditional search engines (Google, Bing) and generative AI engines (ChatGPT Search, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Copilot). Their duties include JSON-LD entity graph engineering, semantic vector clustering, LLM crawler access audits (GPTBot/ClaudeBot), and information gain content formatting.

How is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) different from traditional SEO? ▼

Traditional SEO focuses on keyword density, metadata tweaks, and obtaining backlinks to rank blue links on page 1. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on semantic entity associations, structured fact triples, and authoritative consensus so AI models retrieve, digest, and cite your brand as an authoritative reference in conversational answers.

How do AI engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity decide which brands to cite? ▼

Generative AI models use neural retrieval (RAG) and dense vector embeddings. They prioritize pages that have high information gain, distinct factual consensus across multiple verified independent sources, structured schema markup (JSON-LD), and low reading ambiguity.
