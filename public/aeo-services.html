Common Questions

Answer Engine Optimization FAQ

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring web copy and JSON-LD schema so conversational AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) and voice assistants (Siri, Alexa) cite your brand as the direct response to user questions.

How is AEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO targets blue organic links in a list of results. AEO targets Position Zero—the single extracted answer box, voice response, or LLM citation summary.

Can AEO help with traffic lost to Google AI Overviews?

Yes. When Google provides direct AI Overviews, standard informational clicks drop. AEO positions your domain as the primary linked source embedded directly inside that AI Overview box.
